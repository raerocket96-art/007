# V21 Five-feature gameplay pass

Added five focused features without changing the core free-text mission loop:

1. **Completed Run Archive** – finished branches are automatically stored locally with film, Bond preference, turns and final score.
2. **Field Notes** – private per-session notebook for hypotheses, names, plans and reminders. Autosaves locally and does not silently affect AI state.
3. **Intel Integrity Check** – flags likely stale/contradictory uncertain intel when it overlaps strongly with already confirmed intel.
4. **Best / Worst Decisions** – Final Debrief now extracts the three highest and three lowest scored major decisions.
5. **Operation DNA** – Final Debrief summarizes the run across objective focus, intelligence instinct, discretion, asset protection, proportionality and improvisation.
