# Context-Aware Debrief Update

Updated the AI and procedural debrief logic so decisions are judged from information available at the turn, not hindsight.

Key changes:
- Hidden countdowns, undisclosed safe routes, EOD options, and future consequences may not be assumed.
- Unknown time pressure around catastrophic threats now counts as legitimate urgency.
- Emergency high-risk actions are distinguished from gratuitous/reckless choices.
- Shooting an external lock/barrier is not automatically treated as shooting the explosive mechanism unless the record establishes direct exposure.
- Safer alternatives are penalized only when they were actually available and time allowed.
- Procedural fallback scoring now gives context credit for urgent threats and barrier-access actions while still retaining residual-risk concerns.
