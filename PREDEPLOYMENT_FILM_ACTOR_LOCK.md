# Pre-Deployment Film + Bond Lock

The MI6 secure entry terminal now requires two explicit selections before the rest of the interface unlocks:

1. James Bond film operation
2. Preferred James Bond actor/doctrine

Until both are confirmed:
- Destination Matrix is visually and functionally locked.
- Secure Access / Launch Terminal is locked.
- Route Preview is disabled.
- Credentials cannot be submitted.

After both are selected, the user can enter the MI6 system. The chosen film template is passed directly into the Mission Simulator and the chosen Bond doctrine is persisted as the pre-deployment preference used by the final AI Bond Persona verdict.

Entering through this launch flow clears an old active-mission autosave so a previous run cannot silently override the newly selected film/actor combination. Manual save slots are not touched.
